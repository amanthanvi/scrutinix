# Scrutinix

Public multi-signal URL threat scanner. Glossary for product and design language after the 2026-07-17 redesign grill.

## Language

**Scanner job**:
The core product act: paste a URL (or short batch), stream eight signals, read a verdict with confidence and caveats.
_Avoid_: Investigation, case, analysis session (when meaning the product act)

**Scanner-first**:
Home IA with a narrow brand/story column and a wide scan console; the console is the product.
_Avoid_: Marketing-first hero, dashboard-first chrome

**Costume class**:
A banned visual metaphor that dresses the tool as theater (terminal cockpit, forensic dossier, etc.) instead of a sharp everyday product.
_Avoid_: Theme, skin, aesthetic lane (when meaning banned cosplay)

**Tasteful radical**:
Bold identity through composition, type scale/weight contrast, and color conviction, while keeping familiar paste/analyze/stream/history affordances.
_Avoid_: Invented controls, costume theater, wow motion

**Mail-forwarder scene**:
Mid-afternoon at a bright ordinary desk; an anxious person pastes a sketchy link before forwarding mail and needs calm certainty in seconds.
_Avoid_: SOC analyst scene, vault/forensics scene, cyberpunk operator scene
