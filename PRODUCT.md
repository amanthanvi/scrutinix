# Scrutinix — product (design register)

FOSS public URL threat analyzer. Users paste a link, watch multi-signal results stream, and get a verdict they can trust enough to decide whether to open or forward.

## Who / when

Someone at an ordinary desk about to forward mail — not a SOC analyst in a vault. Anxious; needs calm certainty in seconds.

## What the UI is

A sharp everyday **product tool** (impeccable product register): bold visual identity, boring interaction grammar. Scanner-first home. No costume theater.
